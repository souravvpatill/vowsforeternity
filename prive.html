The Most Private Engagement of Vows for Eternity

Some searches require more than introductions. They require trusted representation.

Vows for Eternity Privé is our most private engagement, created for individuals and families whose lives demand exceptional discretion, considered judgment, and a search conducted with the same care they apply to every other important decision.

Every search is personally overseen by our Executive Team and guided by the insight of our Founder, Anuradda Gupta, whose work over the past fifteen years has earned the trust of accomplished individuals and families across more than sixty-five countries.

There are no algorithms. No predetermined timelines. No introduction targets. Every decision is made deliberately, and every introduction reflects genuine alignment.

When your life is unlike most, your search should be too.
